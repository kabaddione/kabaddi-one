❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the overlay compatible with Streamlabs and XSplit?

Yes! Because the overlay is a standard web page, it works flawlessly with any broadcasting software that supports a "Browser Source" or "Web Source", including OBS Studio, Streamlabs Desktop, XSplit, and vMix.

Will the overlay cause lag on my computer?

Not at all. The overlay is incredibly lightweight. All the heavy lifting (animations, graphics, data syncing) is handled efficiently by the embedded browser engine in OBS, using less than 2% of your CPU.

Do I need a second monitor or phone?

No. You can run the KabaddiOne app on a phone or tablet to control the score, and the overlay will update on your PC in real-time. It's the perfect setup for solo streamers.

Does the overlay include audio sound effects?

Yes. The overlay has built-in audio triggers for raids, super tackles, and match ends. Make sure to check the "Control audio via OBS" box in your Browser Source properties.
