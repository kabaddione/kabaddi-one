Getting a professional broadcast overlay running is easier than most streamers think. In fact, if you follow these steps, you can be on-air in under 2 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Match

Open the KabaddiOne app, tap "Create Match," and enter the team names. Tap "Start Broadcast" to generate your unique overlay URL.

## Step 2: Add a Browser Source in OBS

- Open OBS Studio
- Click the **+** button under "Sources"
- Select **Browser**
- Paste the URL from the app
- Set width to 1920 and height to 1080
- Click OK

## Step 3: Go Live!

That's it! Your overlay is now live. Scores, timers, and animations will update automatically as you score the match in the app.

Pro tip: Check "Control audio via OBS" in the Browser Source properties to hear the raid and tackle sound effects.
