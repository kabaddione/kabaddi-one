With his team down to two men, Pardeep single-handedly kept them alive with a brilliant 3-point raid.

## 4. The Toe Touch Masterclass (Season 6 Final)

In the season final, Pardeep delivered a clinical toe touch that broke the opposition's spirit.

## 3. The 5-Point Super Raid (Season 7)

A super raid that included a dubki, a hand touch, and a back kick in a single raid.

## 2. The Last-Second Escape (Season 8)

With the match tied and time expiring, Pardeep scored the winning point.

## 1. The Legendary 6-Point Raid (Season 4)

This was the raid that made everyone believe Pardeep was the greatest raider of his generation.
