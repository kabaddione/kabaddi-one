Modern kabaddi analytics goes far beyond simply counting raid points and tackle points. Here are the key metrics every serious fan should understand.

## Raid Success Rate

The percentage of successful raids out of total raid attempts. A good raider has a 40%+ success rate.

## Strike Rate

Raid points scored per raid attempt, converted to a percentage.

## Tackle Success Rate

The percentage of successful tackles out of total tackle attempts.

## Total Points Contribution

A player's total points (raid + tackle + bonus) divided by matches played.

## Do-or-Die Raid Percentage

The percentage of do-or-die raids that result in points. This is the most important stat for pressure players.
