KabaddiOne's Bonus Point Alert system automatically displays a high-energy graphic whenever a bonus point is scored.

## How to Enable It

1. Open the KabaddiOne app and start your match
2. Tap the "Overlay Settings" icon (top right)
3. Toggle "Bonus Point Alerts" to ON
4. Go to your OBS overlay — it will update automatically

## Customizing the Animation

You can choose from 4 different bonus point animations in the settings. Choose the one that best fits your broadcast style.

## When It Triggers

The animation triggers automatically when you tap the "Bonus Point" button in the app during a raid.
