We're thrilled to announce the release of KabaddiOne v3.0 — our biggest update yet.

## What's New

- **10 New Overlay Animations** — Super Raid, Super Tackle, All Out, and more
- **Player Profiles** — Detailed stats tracking for every player on your roster
- **Team Management** — Create and manage team rosters, colors, and logos
- **Redesigned Control Panel** — Faster scoring, bigger buttons, one-handed operation
- **Broadcast History** — Replay or re-stream past matches

## How to Update

The update is rolling out to all users this week. Update from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
