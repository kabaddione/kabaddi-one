Raiding is an art form. The best raiders in the world don't just rely on speed — they combine footwork, timing, and tactical awareness to consistently score points.

## 1. The Toe Touch

The simplest and most effective raid. A quick tap of your toe against the defender's foot before retreating.

## 2. The Back Kick

When surrounded, a well-timed back kick to the nearest defender creates an escape route.

## 3. The Hand Touch

A classic technique where the raider lunges forward to touch the defender's hand or arm.

## 4. The Dubki

Duck under the defender's grasp and emerge behind them to score an easy point.

## 5. The Running Hand Touch

A continuous running hand touch that's difficult to counter.
