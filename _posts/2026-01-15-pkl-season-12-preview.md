Pro Kabaddi League Season 12 promises to be the most competitive edition yet, with all 12 franchises loading up on talent during the off-season auctions. From record-breaking bids to fresh coaching appointments, the build-up has been nothing short of electric.

## Teams to Watch

**Patna Pirates** enter the season as defending champions but will face stiff challenges from a rebuilt Bengaluru Bulls squad and a dangerous Jaipur Pink Panthers side. Meanwhile, the new-look Telugu Titans are the dark horses everyone should be watching.

## Key Transfers & Signings

- Star raider Pardeep Narwal moves to U Mumba in a blockbuster trade
- Defensive stalwart Fazel Atrachali joins Puneri Paltan
- Young sensation Ashu Malik promoted to captain at Dabang Delhi

## Our Predictions

We expect the final to be a rematch of last year, but with the outcome flipped. Watch for the emergence of three breakout young players — and one retirement announcement that will shock fans.
