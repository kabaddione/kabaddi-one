In a major development for the sport, the International Olympic Committee (IOC) has confirmed it will formally review kabaddi's inclusion as a demonstration sport at the 2032 Brisbane Olympics.

## What This Means

If approved, kabaddi would join a growing list of sports seeking to expand their global footprint. The review will consider viewership data, player participation numbers, and broadcast appeal.

## Reaction from the Kabaddi Community

"This is a historic moment for our sport," said the International Kabaddi Federation president. "We have been working towards this goal for over a decade."

A final decision is expected by late 2026.
