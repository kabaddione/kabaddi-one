The Women's Kabaddi League is back and bigger than ever. Season 3 will feature eight teams (up from six), a ₹5 crore prize pool, and a landmark broadcast deal with a major sports network.

## Expansion Teams

Two new franchises join the league this season: the Hyderabad Hawks and the Kerala Storm.

## Prize Money

The champion team will walk away with ₹2 crore, while the runner-up takes home ₹1 crore.

## Schedule

The season begins March 20 and runs for five weeks, with matches broadcast in five languages.
