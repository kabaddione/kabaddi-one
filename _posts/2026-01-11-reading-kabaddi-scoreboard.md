Kabaddi's scoreboard can look confusing at first glance. Here's a quick breakdown of the key elements.

## Score

The most important element — the total points each team has scored.

## Raid Timer

Each raid has a 30-second timer. If the raider doesn't score within 30 seconds, the raid is deemed unsuccessful.

## Timeouts Remaining

Each team gets 2 timeouts per half, each lasting 30 seconds.

## Player Tracker

Shows which players are currently on the mat for each team. Players who have been tackled or raided out are "out" until the next all-out.

## Bonus Points

Bonus points are awarded to raiders who touch a specific bonus line in the opponent's half.
