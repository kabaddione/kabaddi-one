Five years ago, if you wanted to watch a local kabaddi match, you'd have to be there in person. Today, thousands of matches are streamed live every week on YouTube, Twitch, and Facebook — and the numbers are only growing.

## The Democratization of Broadcast

Broadcast equipment used to cost tens of thousands of dollars. Now, with just a smartphone, an OBS overlay, and a data connection, anyone can broadcast a professional-looking match.

## Why It Matters

Kabaddi is one of the fastest-growing sports in the world, particularly in South Asia and the Middle East. Streaming is bringing the sport to audiences that mainstream broadcasters have long ignored.

## What's Next?

We expect to see professional streamers begin signing exclusive deals with leagues, much like how the early YouTubers of the 2000s became mainstream media figures.
