The Amateur Kabaddi Federation of India has announced a 12-member squad for the 2026 Asian Games, with the team mixing experienced campaigners and exciting newcomers.

## The Squad

- Pardeep Narwal (Raider)
- Naveen Kumar (Raider)
- Sachin Tanwar (Raider)
- Ashu Malik (Raider)
- Fazel Atrachali (Defender)
- Nitesh Kumar (Defender)
- Rinku Hooda (Defender)
- Mohit Goyat (Defender)
- Karamjeet Singh (All-rounder)
- Prashanth Kumar (All-rounder)
- Sachin Kumar (All-rounder)
- Ajay Thakur (Captain)

The Asian Games kabaddi event begins October 2nd.
