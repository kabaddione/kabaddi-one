The Kabaddi World Cup 2026 will feature a record 16 nations competing across four groups in this expanded format.

## Group A (India's Group)

- India
- South Korea
- England
- Kenya

## India's Match Schedule

- March 3: India vs Kenya
- March 5: India vs England
- March 7: India vs South Korea

The knockout stage begins March 10 with the final on March 15.
